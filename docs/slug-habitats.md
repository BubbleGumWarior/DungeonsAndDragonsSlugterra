# Slug Habitats & Planet Environments

Companion to [slug-hunt-odds.md](slug-hunt-odds.md). Generated by `scripts/gen_odds.py` from `scripts/habitat_model.py` — edit the model, not this file.

## How a slug's chance is worked out

1. Each **planet** has a set of habitats, each with an abundance from 0 (absent) to 1 (dominant).
2. Each **slug** lists the habitats it can live in, each with an affinity from 0 to 1. Defaults come from its element (e.g. every Water slug wants fresh water, ocean, swamp…) and individual slugs override that where their biology differs (Pressure Tick lives at geysers, Jellyish in the sea, Frostfire where ice meets heat, Meduslug can live without water).
3. The slug's **habitat score** is its best `affinity × abundance` plus 0.3 × everything else it can use. If the score is under 0.1 the habitat is too marginal to keep a population alive and the slug is **0%** on that planet.
4. On **airless** worlds, open-air habitats (bare rock, shadow, starlight, radiation) are exposed to vacuum, so only vacuum-hardy slugs (Metal, Energy, Dark, Light, Earth, plus a few) can use them; everything else is limited to the pressurised habitats (settlement, industry, forge, cisterns, gardens, wrecks).
5. Chance = (habitat score ^ 1.6) × a rarity weight, then every planet's column is scaled to total 100%. The exponent favours specialists in a planet's signature habitat over slugs scraping by in a minor one.
