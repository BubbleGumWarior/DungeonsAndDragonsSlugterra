# The Three Clusters

*Common knowledge across the galaxy — what any traveler, trader, or captive would know about the powers that run it.*

---

## The Convergence Cycle

None of the major powers hold territory the way old maps imply. Each cluster of worlds orbits a shared gravitational anchor in a long, elongated cycle known as the **Great Year**. For roughly half of it — **the Verge** — a cluster's planets swing close together, travel between them is fast, and their combined strength is hard to challenge. For the other half — **the Drift** — the same worlds fling far apart along their orbits, communication lags, and each planet is largely on its own. Every cluster runs on this rhythm, though not necessarily in sync with one another.

---

## The Threxa Combine — the Thresh Expanse

Orbiting a dying red giant. The Combine built its wealth and reputation on two things: the Yoke — a discipline that lets a Threxan fully bind and control a bonded slug — and the hunting culture that grew out of it. Civilian slug ownership is banned throughout their territory; Yoked slugs run their industry, their labor, and their military. They are widely feared, openly transactional, and will do business with almost anyone who can pay.

- **Kessa-9** — an open grassland world kept deliberately low-tech, used to host sanctioned hunts.
- **Cindrath** — a volcanic world housing Threxa's fleet command and the training grounds for their Wardens.
- **Ashfall Rift** — a mountainous, geothermal-rich world worked by an occupied labor population.
- **Pyrsis Barrens** — a harsh geyser world used almost exclusively as a labor camp.
- **The Wellspring** — the Combine's capital, built into vast, ancient ruins far older than the Combine itself. Home to the Grand Coliseum, where hunts across the Expanse are broadcast.
- **Vorn's Forge** — a shipyard world where captured slugs are processed and bonded at industrial scale.

## The Verrin Concord — the Lattice

Orbiting a close binary star pair. The Concord isn't a military power — it's a trade alliance, plural and pragmatic, that does business with everyone, Threxa included, and stays carefully neutral in anything it calls "internal" to another power's affairs. Wealth and access matter more here than ideology.

- **Maren's Deep** — an ocean world of floating city-states, home to a long-settled human population with its own government and history.
- **Aurelia Docks** — a sprawling shipyard-and-market world considered neutral ground; representatives of every power, along with independent traders and smugglers, do business here openly.
- **Solenne** — an agricultural world supplying much of the Lattice's food.
- **Kethrun Reach** — a finance and shipping-law world, the place to go for contracts, records, and ownership disputes.
- **Virid Canopy** — a dense jungle world held as a Concord resource concession, its canopy forests worked for timber and rare compounds.

## The Kaeth Dominion — the Ironvault

Orbiting the accretion disc of a black hole. The Dominion is a militarist, expansionist power, openly rivalrous with Threxa over territory and resources rather than ideology. Their economy runs on conscription and total-war production rather than slugs or trade.

- **Kaeloth Prime** — an icebound homeworld and military capital.
- **Drennow Yards** — an asteroid-belt complex building the Dominion's warships.
- **Marrek's Hold** — a swamp world under Dominion military occupation.
- **Ossuary Station** — a fortified outpost watching the border with Threxa space.
- **Vhalden** — a conscript training world.

## Unclaimed Space

- **The Threshold** — a vast derelict shipyard graveyard drifting in open space, claimed by no power. Scavengers and outcasts from every cluster live here, and none of the three factions polices it directly.
