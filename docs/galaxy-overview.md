# Galaxy Overview: The Three Clusters

Setting reference for a Slugterra-flavored D&D campaign set in space. It covers the galaxy's structure, its three rival powers, and all 17 charted worlds.

Spoiler note: this is the revealed, Slugterra-aware setting (slugs, slingers, blasters, space travel). Before the DM's reveal, players are meant to believe the campaign is an ordinary modern-day Earth game, so do not show this to players or use it in player-facing text until the reveal.

---

## How the galaxy works: the Great Year

No power holds territory the way old maps imply. Each cluster of worlds orbits a shared gravitational anchor on a long, elongated cycle called the **Great Year**.

- **The Verge** is roughly half the cycle. A cluster's planets swing close together, travel between them is fast, and their combined strength is hard to challenge.
- **The Drift** is the other half. The same worlds fling far apart along their orbits, communication lags, and each planet is largely on its own.

Every cluster runs on this rhythm, but not necessarily in sync with the others.

There are three clusters, each its own star system, plus one patch of unclaimed space. 17 worlds in total: 6 Threxa, 5 Verrin, 5 Kaeth, 1 Unclaimed.

## 1. The Threxa Combine ("the Thresh Expanse")

- **Star:** a dying red giant.
- **Character:** feared, openly transactional, a hunting culture. It will do business with almost anyone who can pay.
- **Defining trait:** **the Yoke**, a discipline that lets a Threxan fully bind and control a bonded slug.
- **Slug policy:** civilian slug ownership is banned. Yoked slugs run Threxa's industry, labor and military.
- **Rivalry:** the Kaeth Dominion is openly rivalrous with Threxa over territory and resources, not ideology.

Worlds, inner to outer by heat and livability:

| World | Description |
|---|---|
| **Cindrath** | Volcanic world of black-glass ridges and slow rivers of lava. Houses Threxa's fleet command and the training grounds for its Wardens. Recruits drill on obstacle courses over cooling flow fields. Command spires rise from the ash-fall, lit orange from below. Nothing grows here. |
| **Pyrsis Barrens** | Flat, scalding expanse of geysers and mineral crust. Used almost exclusively as a labor camp. Steam swallows sight and sound past a few dozen metres and the ground is never quite solid. Nobody is sent here who has a choice. |
| **Ashfall Rift** | Steep, geothermal-scarred mountains venting steam from fissures that never close. An occupied labor population works the mineral seams under Threxa oversight, with terraces and rope-bridges strung between ridgelines. The air tastes of sulfur. |
| **Vorn's Forge** | Shipyard world of industrial scale: hulls under construction, cargo lifters, and processing floors where captured slugs are bonded and Yoked before shipping out across the Expanse. The work and noise never stop. |
| **The Wellspring** | The Combine's capital, built into vast stone ruins far older than Threxa whose builders nobody remembers. At its heart is the **Grand Coliseum**, where sanctioned hunts and duels are broadcast across the Expanse. Power here runs on spectacle as much as on the Yoke. |
| **Kessa-9** | Open grassland under a dim red sky, kept deliberately free of roads, towers and permanent settlement. Policed just enough to keep the hunting grounds pristine. Sanctioned hunting parties come and go. Wild slugs run in numbers found almost nowhere else in the Expanse. |

## 2. The Verrin Concord ("the Lattice")

- **Star:** a close binary pair.
- **Character:** a plural, pragmatic **trade alliance**, not a military power. It does business with everyone, Threxa included.
- **Politics:** stays carefully neutral in anything it calls "internal" to another power's affairs. Wealth and access matter more than ideology.

Worlds, inner to outer by how much they rely on the binary's light and warmth:

| World | Description |
|---|---|
| **Virid Canopy** | Dense jungle world held as a Concord resource concession, worked for timber and rare compounds found nowhere else. Extraction crews clear paths that the forest reclaims within a season. |
| **Solenne** | Wide cultivated fields and orchard terraces under soft double daylight. Supplies much of the Lattice's food. Unhurried, practical, quietly keeps the alliance fed. |
| **Maren's Deep** | Ocean world with no true land. A long-settled human population lives in floating city-states with their own government, history and old grudges, independent of the Concord that nominally hosts them. |
| **Aurelia Docks** | Sprawling shipyard-and-market world treated as **neutral ground by every power**. Threxan agents, Dominion officers, Concord traders, independents and smugglers all do business in the open. Anything that can be bought, sold or smuggled passes through eventually. |
| **Kethrun Reach** | World of towers, ledgers and courts. The place for contracts, shipping law, records and ownership disputes across the Lattice. Nothing moves without paperwork. Scrupulously, coldly fair, which is its own kind of ruthless. |

## 3. The Kaeth Dominion ("the Ironvault")

- **Star:** the accretion disc of a black hole.
- **Character:** a **militarist, expansionist** power.
- **Economy:** runs on conscription and total-war production, not on slugs or trade.
- **Rivalry:** openly rivalrous with Threxa over territory and resources.

Worlds, inner to outer by proximity to the accretion disc's heat:

| World | Description |
|---|---|
| **Marrek's Hold** | Swamp world under open Dominion military occupation, threaded with patrol causeways and checkpoint towers. The air is thick, warm and faintly toxic. Locals know exactly how far they're allowed to go. |
| **Drennow Yards** | Asteroid-belt complex of docking scaffolds and foundries, building the Dominion's warships. **Airless.** |
| **Vhalden** | Harsh, wind-scoured training world where conscripts are broken down and rebuilt into soldiers. Obstacle ranges scar the terrain for kilometres, and the freezing night counts as part of the curriculum. |
| **Ossuary Station** | Fortified outpost bristling with sensors and gun emplacements, watching the border with Threxa space around the clock. Named for what was found drifting nearby when the Dominion claimed the position. **Airless.** |
| **Kaeloth Prime** | Icebound homeworld and military capital under the pale, restless light of the accretion disc. Cities are low and armored against the cold. Every street answers to military command and every child expects conscription. Glacier-blue, silent, absolutely disciplined. |

## Unclaimed Space

| World | Description |
|---|---|
| **The Threshold** | Huge graveyard of derelict hulls drifting in open space between the clusters. Claimed by no power and policed by none. Scavengers, outcasts and people from all three clusters who ran out of options live here, picking the wrecks for parts and stories. The old rules don't apply. It sits outside the Great Year cycle and drifts on its own. **Airless.** |

## Slug ecology by world (game mechanics)

Which slugs can be found on which world is decided by a habitat model. Every world has habitats such as magma, ocean, forest, ruins, wreck and vacuum, and every slug has habitats it can live in.

Highlights:

- **Cindrath:** about a third Fire slugs. No Plant, Ice, Water or Healing.
- **Kessa-9, Virid Canopy, Solenne:** Plant and Healing slugs thrive. Kessa-9 has no Fire.
- **Maren's Deep:** the best place for Water slugs (about 14%) and one of the few places for Ice.
- **Kaeloth Prime:** about a quarter Ice.
- **Vorn's Forge and Aurelia Docks:** Metal and Electricity.
- **Kethrun Reach and The Wellspring:** Psychic.
- **Airless worlds (Drennow Yards, Ossuary Station, The Threshold):** only vacuum-hardy slugs live outside pressurised structures, mostly Dark, Metal, Energy and Light. Water, Plant, Ice and Healing slugs are essentially absent.

## Notes for an AI using this document

- Use this as canon for names, factions and geography. Do not invent additional worlds, clusters or powers unless asked.
- World names are proper nouns: Cindrath, Pyrsis Barrens, Ashfall Rift, Vorn's Forge, The Wellspring, Kessa-9, Virid Canopy, Solenne, Maren's Deep, Aurelia Docks, Kethrun Reach, Marrek's Hold, Drennow Yards, Vhalden, Ossuary Station, Kaeloth Prime, The Threshold.
- Tone: grounded, a little grim, morally gray. The powers are defined by what they do and trade, not by good versus evil.
- The Great Year (Verge and Drift) is available as a pacing tool. Worlds in the same cluster are close during the Verge and isolated during the Drift.
